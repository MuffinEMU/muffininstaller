Step one

One tap, from the web.

Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad and tap a certificate to install MuffinEMU on-device. Pick one marked valid. No computer, no cable.

Loading certificates…

Each card shows where the build comes from and installs only when you tap it — nothing downloads on its own. Certificates are added by their owners; no stolen or shared keys are listed here.

Step two

Or with your own Apple ID.

Once MuffinEMU is installed, it signs and renews itself with your own Apple ID — the official MuffinInstaller, built into the app. On iOS 27 it pairs on-device; on 17.4–26 you import a pairing file once.

01

Install the app

Tap a certificate above, or add MuffinEMU through SideStore or AltStore.

02

Open MuffinInstaller

In the app, go to Settings › Storage › MuffinInstaller, connect LocalDevVPN, and pair.

03

Sign in & forget it

Sign in with your Apple ID, or use your own certificate. MuffinEMU keeps itself installed and updated — refreshing before a free Apple ID's 7 days run out.

MuffinInstaller and on-device pairing need iOS 27 or later. Everything else — the app itself and JIT with an imported pairing — works on iOS 15 and up.

How it works

Two ways in, one emulator.

From the web

Each certificate below signs the latest MuffinEMU and hands your device an install link. One tap in Safari and it lands on your home screen. Certificates are added by their owners — no stolen or shared keys are listed here.

Your own signing

The in-app MuffinInstaller re-signs MuffinEMU with your Apple ID or your own certificate and keeps it renewed, so it never expires out from under you. Nothing leaves your device except the sign-in Apple already handles.
